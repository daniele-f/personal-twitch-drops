Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data?

Unidea operates Personal Twitch Drops and is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact privacy@unidea.dev.

What we collect and why

If you create an account, we process your email address and authentication information so that you can sign in, secure your account, reset your password, and sync your tracker preferences between devices. If you choose Twitch sign-in, we process the Twitch identity information provided for authentication.

We also store the tracker preferences you choose to sync, such as favorite and ignored games, collected rewards, and display preferences. We process this data to provide the account and sync features you request. We may process limited technical information needed to secure and operate the service.

Service providers

We use Supabase to provide authentication and store synced account data. We use Cloudflare to host and deliver the service. The app requests drop information from twitchdrops.app and may request game-link information from Wikidata; those services receive the technical data needed to respond to your browser request, and Wikidata receives the game name used for the lookup. If you use Twitch sign-in, Twitch processes authentication through its service. These providers process personal data only as needed to provide their services.

How long we keep data

We keep account and synced tracker data while your account remains active. You can request deletion of your account and associated synced data by emailing privacy@unidea.dev. We may retain limited data where necessary for security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of your personal data, and you may object to certain processing. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority. To make a request, contact privacy@unidea.dev.

Cookies

We use essential storage needed to keep you signed in and provide the service. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy and request consent where required.
